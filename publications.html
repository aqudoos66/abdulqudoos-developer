Peer-Reviewed Journal Article November 2025

"Smart Conference Management System (SCMS)"

Abdul Qudoos, Abdul Khaliq, Dr. Aijaz Ahmed Arain, Muhammad Furqan, Shahzad Ali
International Journal of Innovations in Science & Technology (IJIST), Vol. 7, Issue 10, pp. 10–22. Presented at the 1st International Conference on Computing Sciences and Emerging Trends (CSET'26).

Abstract: Traditional conference management workflows suffer from fragmented communication channels, manual paper distribution delays, and repetitive website setup overhead. The Smart Conference Management System (SCMS) provides an integrated platform that automates conference landing page generation, paper submission tracking, intelligent reviewer matching, and real-time communication via automated email and WhatsApp webhooks. Testing across simulated academic conference benchmarks demonstrated a 98% reduction in website deployment time, a 98% notification delivery rate, and an overall user satisfaction rating of 4.7 out of 5.

Research Impact & Technical Architecture

The SCMS project was designed and built to validate how modern web engineering patterns (Laravel MVC, indexed relational MySQL tables, background queues, and LLM-assisted paper metadata extraction) can modernize academic operations that historically relied on expensive proprietary commercial software.
